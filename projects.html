Banking ALM, IRRBB & FTP Regulatory Engine

Sep 2026 – Present

Building a tool in Python and SQL that shows how interest-rate changes affect a bank's profits and balance sheet, following international banking rules (Basel III) and Vietnamese regulations. It runs on real data from Vietnamese bank reports and government bonds.

I'm also adding modules that break down the bank's funding costs by product and track cash-flow risks, with dashboards showing how the bank would hold up under stressed scenarios.

U.S. Treasury Yield Event Study

Sep 2026 – Present

Studied how 94 major economic announcements (inflation reports and Federal Reserve decisions) moved U.S. government bond yields on the day they were released. Yields swing far more on announcement days than ordinary days, showing how central-bank decisions ripple through to borrowing costs for businesses and households.
